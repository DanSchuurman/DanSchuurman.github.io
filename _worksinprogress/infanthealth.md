We estimate the causal effects of pesticide exposure on prenatal health in California. We assemble an individual-level dataset from 2007 to 2017 linking birth outcomes to daily local pesticide exposure. We use quasi-experimental variation in exposure via pesticide drift using local wind direction on day-of-application. Finally, our study considers the demographic distribution of exposure and health outcomes.
